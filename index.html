The Journey So Far.

My work in systems, development, and team environments has helped me build solid experience across technical and collaborative roles.

  • Defence Research and Development Laboratory (DRDL), DRDO

    Software Engineering Intern (2026)

    Designed and developed a remote HPC job execution and monitoring system that enables users to submit, monitor, and troubleshoot computational jobs running on remote Linux servers over SSH. The system supports PBS/TORQUE-compatible scheduling, live job monitoring, and intelligent diagnostics to simplify interaction with command-line HPC environments. Working in a research environment reinforced the importance of reliability, maintainability, and understanding systems beyond just writing code.

  • Tata Advanced Systems Limited (TASL)

    Engineering Intern

    Introduced to defence engineering workflows and Linux-based radar system testing. Working with mission-critical systems taught me to learn quickly, adapt to unfamiliar technologies, and appreciate the discipline required when software interacts with real-world hardware.
